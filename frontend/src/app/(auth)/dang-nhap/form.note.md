  - forgot: `POST /api/auth/forgot-password` body `{ email }`
- Backend:
  - `AuthController.login`
  - `AuthController.forgotPassword`
  - `AuthService`, `EmailService`
- DB:

  - `vai_tro`
  - `nguoi_dung_vai_tro`

  - On forgot password ok, backend sends new password/reset email flow.
